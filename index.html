Frequently Asked Questions

What types of photography services do you offer?

We specialize in wedding photography, corporate events, lifestyle shoots, and special occasions. Our team is experienced in various photography styles including portrait, documentary, and artistic photography.

How do you handle the editing process?

Our editing process includes color correction, exposure adjustment, and retouching. We maintain a consistent style while ensuring each image reflects the true essence of the moment. We provide a mix of color and black & white images based on the event's requirements.

What equipment do you use for shoots?

We use professional-grade Canon and Sony cameras with a variety of lenses to capture different perspectives. Our equipment includes lighting setups, stabilizers, and backup gear to ensure we never miss a moment.

How do you handle outdoor shoots in different weather conditions?

We're prepared for all weather conditions with appropriate equipment protection and backup plans. For outdoor shoots, we monitor weather forecasts and can reschedule if necessary. We also have experience creating beautiful images in various lighting conditions.

What is your approach to capturing candid moments?

We believe in capturing authentic, unposed moments while maintaining a professional presence. Our photographers are skilled at blending into the background while being ready to capture those special, spontaneous moments that make your event unique.
