1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Andy-B Studios (e.g., book a session, contact us, or browse our site), we may collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Booking details
  • Messages or special requests

We may also collect anonymous data such as browser type, device, and usage stats via cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process bookings and respond to inquiries
  • Communicate updates, confirmations, or service changes
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send promotional content (with your consent)

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Analyze website traffic
  • Understand user behavior
  • Remember your preferences

By using our site, you consent to our use of cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. However, we may share limited data with trusted third parties to:

  • Process payments
  • Send emails (e.g., newsletter services)
  • Provide analytics and performance tracking

These third parties are bound by privacy agreements and only use your data as needed.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct or update your information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@andybstudios.com

7. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter or promotional updates, you can opt out at any time using the "unsubscribe" link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy regularly.
